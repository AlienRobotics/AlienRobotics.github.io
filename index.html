    Excubitor

    Next-Generation Defense Technology

    Excubitor — our first system — is an autonomous turret that detects and neutralizes fast-moving aerial and ground targets at a fraction of the cost of an incoming drone swarm. Future versions deploy as networks of turrets and supporting modules, fusing data from ground and airborne sensors into coordinated responses across the full system.

    Multi-Target Engagement

    Built for Swarms

    Excubitor handles the full engagement loop autonomously — scanning the scene, identifying every target in frame, prioritizing them by threat, and engaging each in sequence at machine reaction speed.

    The Coordination Platform

    More than a turret.

    Excubitor is the first module. The next step is a network platform that coordinates distributed inputs — video, infrared, and human speech — scaling one turret into a defense against an entire swarm.
